# Trend research

Goal: pick the topic with the best chance of being watched to the end and shared, using evidence from this week, not memory.

## Rules

- Always search before choosing. The date is in the conversation; use it in queries ("2026" or "this week"), never a stale year.
- Run separate searches for separate angles. One combined query returns shallow results.
- Prefer sources from the last 14 days. Note the date of each source you rely on.
- Treat "trending" lists on SEO sites with suspicion. Cross-check a trend in at least two independent places before building on it.
- Do not claim to know Instagram's live trending data. Say what the sources indicate and how recent they are.

## Search plan

Run these in one batch, adapting the niche words to the user's channel:

1. Instagram Reels trends this week: formats, editing styles, audio trends.
2. Viral short video topics this week in the user's niches (technology, AI, cybersecurity, careers, education, daily habits).
3. News from the last 7 days in those niches: product launches, outages, breaches, policy changes, big announcements.
4. What people in Uzbekistan are discussing now: local news, exams and admissions, jobs, exchange rates, mobile apps and services, public holidays and seasonal events.
5. Seasonal and calendar hooks in the next two weeks: holidays, exam periods, school year milestones, sales events.
6. If the user wants a series topic: evergreen questions people keep asking in the niche, from search suggestions and forums.

Fetch the two or three strongest pages in full rather than relying on snippets.

## Candidate scorecard

Build five candidates. Score each from 0 to 5 on each row. Add the scores.

| Criterion | What a 5 looks like |
|---|---|
| Timeliness | Something happened in the last 7 days, or a date makes it relevant now |
| Broad relevance | Almost anyone watching can say "that is about me" in the first two seconds |
| Surprise or tension | Contains a counterintuitive fact, a mistake people make, or an open question |
| Shareability | Someone would send it to a friend with "look at this" |
| Producibility | Can be made well with the user's tools and style within one session |
| Freshness for the channel | Not made before, see `house-style.md` |
| Truthfulness | Facts are solid and can be sourced |

Winner needs the highest total and no zero in Truthfulness or Producibility.

## Angle selection

A good topic can still fail with a flat angle. For the winner, write the angle as one sentence in this shape:

"Everyone thinks X, but actually Y, and here is what to do."

Alternatives: the mistake everyone makes, the thing nobody tells you, the 10 second test, before and after, a 3 step answer to a question people are really asking.

## Output of this stage

A short table of the five candidates with total scores and dates of the supporting sources, the winner, the angle sentence, and the source links for any fact the script will use. Keep the runner up options visible in one line.
