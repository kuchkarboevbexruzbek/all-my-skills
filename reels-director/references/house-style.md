# House style: this channel

Update this file as the channel grows. It records what the creator likes, what went wrong in earlier renders, and which topics are already made, so every new Reel starts from what was learned.

## Creator and channel

- Creator: Bexruzbek, developer from Tashkent. Social handle on his short videos: @kuchqarboev.
- Language: Uzbek (Latin script). Always the formal "siz" form toward the audience, for example "bilmasangiz". Never the informal "sen".
- Goal: useful or educational short videos on topics that are relevant to everyone, made to be interesting and shareable.
- Recurring main character: Bexruzbek himself (earlier the character was called Aziz). He wants a different visual style from reel to reel.

## How the creator works

1. The script is written first, together with Claude. The creator often finalizes the script himself.
2. Claude writes the image prompts. The creator generates the images in Gemini one by one and uploads them.
3. The creator records his own voice (or uses ElevenLabs voices with audio tags like `[excited]`, `[reassuring]`) and sends numbered files.
4. Claude builds and renders the final Remotion video itself, with subtitles and animations. The creator wants to be told when it is ready.
5. Sometimes the work is split across several Claude accounts: one writes each character's code, then the parts are merged step by step.

## Styles used so far

- 2D illustrated sticker and collage style, Uzbek subtitles.
- Presenter cut-out with animated text and infographics.
- 3D low poly Three.js sketches: Bexruzbek (short black hair, rimless glasses, black open shirt over a white tee, black trousers), a "Claude" robot that is caring and attentive (small squinting dot eyes, never angry looking), and a "Codex" robot that is hasty. Each has a different voice. Dialogues are everyday situations that are relatable yet surprising.
- Character image convention: sticker style cartoon on a green screen with a white outline.

## Lessons from past renders (apply by default)

- Voice plays at 1.25x and the video is matched to that speed.
- No moving progress bar at the top.
- Use only a few decorative elements. For example, 3 or 4 question marks coming out of a character's shoulder, not a flood.
- No text over the character's face.
- An enumeration or list is shown as a plain page that opens, with the items written in by animation and without the character on screen.
- No overlapping elements anywhere.
- Hooks that work: a question in the first line, stress on a key word, a short history beat when it helps, a three step answer, a comment prompt at the end.
- Do not rush a step by step answer. Keep it calm and clear.
- Accent color is green. Never pink.
